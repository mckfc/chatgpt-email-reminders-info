Privacy policy

This policy describes the personal, owner-operated ChatGPT Email Reminders utility and this informational website.

Information the utility accesses

With the owner's Google authorization, the utility accesses selected Gmail message identifiers, labels, sender information, subjects, received times, and eligible message text. It reads the dedicated reminder calendar to avoid duplicates and creates private reminders containing event details and a source email link.

The requested Google permissions are Gmail read-only and access to calendars created by the application. The utility does not request permission to send email.

How information is used

Email information is used to identify important events and actions, apply sender and keyword exclusions, prevent duplicate reminders, and support manual review. Selected eligible email content is reviewed in the owner's ChatGPT/Codex account. That processing and its retention also depend on the settings and terms of that account.

The local utility does not include a separately billed model API integration and does not itself train AI models. Google data is not used by this utility for advertising or sold to others.

Storage and protection

The local runtime stores OAuth authorization and cached message content using platform-protected encryption. Local records retain message identifiers, decisions, review state, and reminder identifiers for deduplication and continuity. Created reminders are stored in the owner's Google Calendar.

The website does not contain the local runtime, OAuth credentials, mailbox data, or calendar data. Email images and attachments are not executed by the utility.

Services involved

Google provides Gmail, Calendar, and OAuth authorization. The owner's ChatGPT/Codex account is used for the review workflow. This informational website may be hosted by GitHub Pages; hosting providers may process ordinary web request information such as IP addresses and access logs under their own privacy policies. There are no analytics scripts, advertising scripts, account forms, or tracking cookies added by this website.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

The owner can revoke Google authorization in Google Account settings and stop the local automation. Local runtime records remain until the owner removes them; revocation alone does not delete cached records or existing calendar reminders. The owner can delete reminders in Google Calendar and manage retention in their ChatGPT/Codex account.
